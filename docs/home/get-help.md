## Before you ask

- Search the [FAQ](faq.md), [Troubleshooting](../operations/troubleshooting.md) and the
  [Discussions](https://github.com/eclipse-tractusx/tractusx-edc/discussions).
- Note your Tractus-X EDC version, how you deploy it (Helm chart version, values) and what you expected.
- Want to contribute a fix? Read [Contributing](../developer/contributing.md).
